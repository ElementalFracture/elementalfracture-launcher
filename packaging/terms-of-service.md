## 1. Who can use this

- You must be at least **13 years old**, or older where your local law or Discord requires. If you're under the age of majority where you live, you should have a parent or guardian's okay.
- Access is a privilege extended by a volunteer community, not a right. We may change, pause, or shut down any part of the Services at any time.

## 2. Your account

- You create an account with a **username, email, and password**, verified by a one-time email code, on the website or in the launcher. Linking Discord is optional.
- **Keep your credentials secret.** Don't share your password or paste it anywhere except the official website, launcher, or game. If it leaks, change it right away.
- **Use an email address you can receive mail at.** You'll need it to verify your account and to recover it. If you typed it wrong, you can change it before you verify.
- You're responsible for what happens on your account.
- **One person, one account.** Don't make extra accounts to dodge moderation, impersonate people, boost stats, or get around a mute or suspension.

## 3. Names

Your username is your **public identity**: other players see it in matches, on scoreboards, in chat, in friends and party lists, on the Matches board, and on the hi-scores (unless you opt out). It has to be fit for everyone.

**Not allowed in a name:**

- Slurs, hate speech, or anything attacking people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any similar trait, including coded or misspelled versions.
- Sexual or sexually suggestive content.
- References to violent extremism, terrorism, or real-world tragedies used for shock value.
- Harassment of, or references to, a specific real person.
- Impersonation of staff, other players, Proletariat, or anyone else.
- Names built to look like system messages, or that are hard to read on purpose.

**What happens:** names are checked automatically when they're chosen and checked again regularly. A name that breaks these rules is **reset** to a placeholder, and you choose a new one on your account page. Staff can also reset a name. Repeatedly choosing names that break these rules is treated as a conduct violation (see section 8). We keep a history of names used on each account so staff can follow an account across name changes.

## 4. Chat

In-game chat is for talking to the people you play with. The same rules apply in every channel (menu, match, squad, private messages, and global), in Discord, and anywhere else the community gathers.

**Not allowed:**

- **Hateful content**: slurs, hate speech, or attacks on anyone for who they are.
- **Harassment and abuse**: insults aimed at a person, threats, bullying, following someone across channels, or encouraging others to pile on.
- **Sexual content**, and anything sexualizing a minor (see section 6).
- **Spam and flooding**: repeating messages, filling the chat, or posting walls of text or symbols.
- **Griefing through chat**: deliberately disrupting other people's games or conversations, baiting, or false reports made to get someone punished.
- **Personal information**: posting anyone's real name, address, contact details, or other private details without their permission (doxxing).
- **Scams and advertising**: phishing, asking for passwords or codes, selling anything, or advertising other servers or services.
- **Impersonation** of staff or other players.

Trash talk about the match is fine. Making it about the person is not.

**Tools you have:** you can block a player, which hides their messages from you and stops them messaging you, and you can choose which channels you see in Settings. Use **Report** for anything that breaks these rules (see section 7).

**Keep in mind:** what you send in chat is delivered to other players' computers. We keep a short chat log for moderation (see the Privacy Policy), but we can't control what other players do with messages they receive, such as screenshots or saved logs.

## 5. Fair play

Battle Royale only works when every player and every team is playing to win on their own. These rules apply in every mode; the Battle Royale specifics come first.

### Battle Royale

- **No teaming.** In Solos, you play alone: no working with another player in any way. In Duos and Squads, you work only with your own team. Agreeing to fight together, deliberately leaving each other alone, sharing information, or trading items with another team is teaming. So is "truces" that start or continue during the match.
- **No ghosting.** Don't use spectating or a stream to pass information to a player who is still in the match.
- **No throwing.** Don't deliberately lose, hand kills or loot to another player, or feed opponents to change who wins.
- **No griefing your own team.** Don't sabotage teammates: no deliberately abandoning them to die, stealing their revives, or blocking them, and no going AFK or leaving to drag a team down on purpose.
- **No win trading or stat boosting.** Don't arrange results, farm kills from a friend's alternate account, or play in any other way that fakes stats, ratings, or rewards.

### Every mode

- **No cheating**: no aimbots, wallhacks, ESP, speed or damage hacks, injected exploits (other than our own sanctioned mods), or memory editing for an advantage.
- **No exploiting bugs** for an unfair edge or to disrupt matches. Report serious exploits to staff instead.
- **No disrupting matches**: no deliberately crashing or lagging servers, and no queue manipulation to land in a match with friends against the matchmaker's teams.
- **No unauthorized automation** of gameplay, queues, or community systems.

### Spectator mode

Spectator mode lets you watch matches in play, from the EF Controller's Matches board or after you're out of a match. It's there so players can **improve by watching other players**. Only players are shown; bots are skipped.

- **Don't use it to harass or stalk** a player, or to follow someone from match to match against their wishes.
- **No ghosting**: don't pass what you see to anyone still in the match.
- **No sniping**: don't watch a player to find them and target them in the same or a later match.

If you'd rather not be watched, **opt out of being spectated** on the website's account page. Spectators then skip you, except your own squad after they're out of the match.

What counts as teaming, throwing, or griefing can depend on the situation. Staff decide case by case, using reports, match records, and recordings (see section 8).

## 6. Be a decent person

These apply everywhere: in game, in chat, in the launcher, on the website, and in Discord.

- **Treat others with respect.** No harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, stalking, or targeted abuse.
- **No sexual content involving minors, ever.** Anything sexualizing anyone under 18, real or fictional, means an immediate permanent ban and, where required, a report to the authorities.
- **Nothing illegal**, and nothing that incites violence, promotes self-harm, or glorifies terrorism or hate.
- **Don't attack our infrastructure or other people's accounts**: no DoS, unauthorized probing, credential theft, or ban evasion.
- **Don't misrepresent the project** as official, and don't use our mods, tools, or accounts against official or live servers of any game.
- **Don't try to monetize** the game, our servers, accounts, items, or access to them. This project is free and stays free.

## 7. Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, **report them**. You can report from the match-end screen, while spectating, from a player's entry in the social menu, or through a Discord ticket.

A report includes the reason you chose, anything you wrote, and context we attach automatically: the match and server it came from, and the recent chat between you and the reported player. Reported Battle Royale matches have their recording kept for longer so staff can review them (see the Privacy Policy).

Report honestly. Reports filed to harass someone, or to get a player punished for something they didn't do, are themselves a violation.

We can't always tell you the outcome of a report, but every report is seen by staff.

## 8. Moderation and enforcement

Staff enforce these Terms. We look at the evidence available: reports, chat logs, match records, match recordings, server records of commands run, and moderation history. We act on the most serious problem first and consider your history. A summary of every report and its outcome stays on your account record, so past offences count toward later decisions.

**What we can do:**

| Action | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Warning** | A recorded notice. No restrictions, but it counts toward your history. |
| **Name reset** | Your name is replaced with a placeholder and you choose a new one. |
| **Chat mute** | You can't send chat messages. Mutes can be temporary or permanent. You can still play. |
| **Suspension** | A temporary ban: you can't sign in or play until the end date shown to you. |
| **Permanent ban** | You can't sign in or play, and you may not return on another account. |

Enforcement usually escalates: a first minor offence typically gets a warning or a short mute, and repeat offences get longer mutes and suspensions. **Serious violations skip the ladder**: cheating, hate speech, threats, sexual content involving minors, doxxing, and attacks on our infrastructure can lead straight to a suspension or a permanent ban.

When you're suspended or banned, you see a message when you try to sign in that says how long it lasts and, where we give one, the reason.

**Ban evasion:** making or using another account to get around a mute, suspension, or ban extends or makes permanent the original action, and the new account is banned too. To stop evasion, enforcement can attach to your account and to network or device identifiers linked to it.

## 9. Appeals

If you think we got it wrong, **appeal**. Open a ticket in our Discord or email **hello@elefrac.com** with:

- your username,
- the action you're appealing (mute, name reset, suspension, or ban) and roughly when it happened,
- why you think it was wrong, and anything that shows it.

Where we can, a staff member who didn't make the original decision reviews the appeal. We'll tell you whether the action stays, changes, or is lifted. **Response times vary**: we're volunteers and can't commit to answering within a set time. Please appeal once per action; repeated appeals or harassing staff about a decision won't speed it up.

You can also stop using the Services and ask us to delete your account at any time (see the Privacy Policy).
