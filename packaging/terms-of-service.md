## 1. Who can use this

- You must be at least **13 years old** (or older where your local law or Discord requires). If you're under the age of majority where you live, you should have a parent or guardian's okay.
- Access is a privilege extended by a volunteer community, not a right. We may change, pause, or shut down any part of the Services at any time.

## 2. Your account

- Accounts are created with a **username, email, and password** (verified by a one-time email code), from the website or the launcher, with **optional Discord linking**. Members who prefer Discord can also register through our bot, "Marla," with `/register`.
- **Keep your credentials secret.** Your password is your key. Don't share it or paste it anywhere except the official launcher or game. If it leaks, change your password right away, and reset it if you're locked out.
- You're responsible for what happens on your account.
- **One person, one identity.** Don't make extra accounts to dodge moderation, impersonate people, or inflate stats.

See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) for exactly what we store.

## 3. Community Guidelines

Elemental Fracture exists because people love this game and want a welcoming place to play it. These apply everywhere the community gathers: in-game, in the launcher, and in Discord.

### Be a decent person

- **Treat others with respect.** No harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, stalking, or targeted abuse. Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any similar trait is not tolerated.
- **No sexual content involving minors, ever.** Anything sexualizing anyone under 18, real or fictional, means an immediate permanent ban and, where required, a report to the authorities.
- **Nothing illegal**, and nothing that incites violence, promotes self-harm, or glorifies terrorism or hate.
- Heated match banter is fine; bullying and cruelty are not.

### Play fair

- **No cheating**: no aimbots, wallhacks, ESP, injected exploits (other than our own sanctioned mods), or memory editing for advantage.
- **No exploiting bugs** for an unfair edge or to disrupt matches. Report serious exploits to staff instead of abusing them.
- **No griefing, match sabotage, or unauthorized botting** of gameplay, queues, or community systems.
- Our servers run on donated time and money. Be considerate of other players and of the servers.

### Respect the community and the mission

- **No impersonation** of staff, other members, Proletariat, Blizzard, or anyone else.
- **No spam, scams, phishing, or advertising.** Never try to trick anyone into handing over a token, password, or code. Ignore and report suspicious DMs.
- **Pick a legible, appropriate name.** No offensive, impersonating, or disruptive names.
- **Don't use our mods, tools, or accounts against official/live servers** of any game. They're for the Elemental Fracture preservation servers only.
- **Don't try to monetize** the game, our servers, or access to them. This project is free and stays free.
- **Don't attack our infrastructure or other people's accounts** (no DoS, unauthorized probing, credential theft, or ban evasion), and don't misrepresent the project as official.
